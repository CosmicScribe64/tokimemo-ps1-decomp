## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Design player appearance/name/gender and forms-of-address options, with identity and appearance independently represented where practical.
- [ ] Inventory all gendered references in dialogue, narration, menus, endings, events, image text and voice; adapt references consistently to the selected player.
- [ ] Use contextual localization variants rather than global pronoun replacement; review relationship and event assumptions without silently changing romance routes.
- [ ] Define needed portrait/sprite/CG and voiced-line alternatives and explicit fallback policy for original recordings that conflict with the selected identity.
- [ ] Persist choices safely and verify representative routes/endings plus save compatibility. Scope and art/voice feasibility need design before implementation.
